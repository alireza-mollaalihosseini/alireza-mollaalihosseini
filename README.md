## Alireza Hosseini

Physicist working on machine learning for audio and sensor data.
Publications list me as *A. Molla Ali Hosseini*.

- Research associate and doctoral researcher at **TU Ilmenau** in
  [NeuroSensEar](https://www.tu-ilmenau.de/en/news/neurosensear-bio-inspired-technology-for-highly-efficient-hearing-aids),
  a Carl Zeiss Foundation project with Fraunhofer IDMT, IMMS and FSU Jena that develops cochlea-inspired
  MEMS sensors for hearing aids
- M.Sc. Physics of Data, University of Padova · B.Sc. Physics, Sharif University of Technology
- Co-author of *From CNN to Reservoir Computing: A New Perspective on Acoustic Scene Classification*,
  AES International Conference on AI and Machine Learning for Audio, London, 2025

**What I work on:** simulation-driven ML pipelines on HPC clusters, evaluation that holds up
(group-disjoint splits, bootstrap confidence intervals), hardware-aware ML for sensors, and nonlinear
dynamics.
